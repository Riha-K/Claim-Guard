# Auto Collision Claims Policy

## Covered events
- Collision with another vehicle while the policy is active
- Single-vehicle accidents involving the insured car (for example, hitting a barrier)
- Partial coverage for towing after a covered collision

## Not covered
- Driving without a valid license
- Accidents under the influence of alcohol or drugs
- Intentional damage
- Mechanical failure unrelated to a collision

## Typical required documents
- Claim form with date, time, and location
- Driving license copy
- Registration certificate (RC)
- FIR or police report when a third party is involved
- Photos of damage and repair estimate

## Decision hints
- Approve for review when documents are complete and no fraud flags fire
- Request documents when checklist items are missing
- Escalate when fraud signals are strong or injury claims are involved
