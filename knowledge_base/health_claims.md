# Health Insurance Claims Policy

## Covered events
- Hospitalization for medically necessary treatment
- Day-care procedures listed in the policy schedule
- Pre and post hospitalization expenses within the stated window

## Waiting periods
- Initial waiting period: 30 days for most illnesses (accidents may be exempt)
- Specific disease waiting periods apply as listed in the schedule
- Pre-existing disease waiting period as per policy year rules

## Typical required documents
- Claim form
- Discharge summary
- Hospital bills and payment receipts
- Investigation reports
- KYC of the insured

## Decision hints
- Approve for medical review when hospitalization proof is present
- Request documents when discharge summary or bills are missing
- Escalate high-value claims above the configured threshold
